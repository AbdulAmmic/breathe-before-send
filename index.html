    Before we turn on your camera

    Breathe Before Send watches for signs that your body is activated while you write, for example a faster pulse or quicker breathing. When you press Send at a moment like that, it offers you a minute to breathe first. You can always send anyway.

    • On your device. SmartSpectra reads pulse, breathing and heart rate variability (and expression, if you allow it) on this computer. This app never saves or uploads video, and Presage documents that the SDK does not send video frames or your readings.
    • Nothing is stored. Readings and your baseline live in memory and disappear when you close the app. Your message text is never sent anywhere.
    • Only you. It reads one face, the person at the keyboard. It pauses if it sees more than one face.
    • Not medical. This is a self-awareness nudge, not a health or stress measurement.
    • The SDK contacts Presage to check the API key and usage, and to download its models. Anonymous performance telemetry (no video, no readings) is off unless you turn it on below.

    Works best with light on your face (not behind you) and your head and shoulders in view.
